How to use the system, after the October update

What changed and how to do each everyday task: customer orders, reserving stock, supplier procurement, receiving goods, Gelco transfers and invoices, documents and finance. Each section says where to find it and who can do it.

Before you start

One-time setup for admins and managers

  1. Set invoice details for items you send to Gelco. Each item needs a rate, an HSN code and the description to print on the invoice. You can set them on the Transfer page the first time you move the item to Gelco Stores, or later in Docs → Sales.
  2. Check that the Tally agent on the office PC is running. Finance shows Tally's data; the latest sales voucher received is from 25 Jun 2026.
  3. Reserve stock for open POs. Open each customer PO on Orders and press Reserve where free stock exists, so what's still short shows up for procurement.

Customer orders

Orders → POs

Every customer PO now shows a status worked out from its lines. Nobody sets it by hand any more. Click a PO number to open its overlay.

Each line shows five figures: Ordered, Dispatched (from real outwards on that PO), Reserved (reels set aside for it), In procurement (on a supplier order, not yet arrived) and Still needed.

Line statuses, least to most advanced

  • Not planned
  • EQ
  • RM Procurement
  • Under Manufacturing
  • Under QC
  • In Transit
  • Ready
  • Dispatch
  • Completed

Ready means reserved stock covers what's left to ship. Dispatch means part has shipped and the rest is reserved. A PO's status is its least advanced line.

The Dispatch tab is still there for planned shipment dates. Actual dispatched quantities come from Outward.

View: admin, manager, staff. Reserve, release and dispatch rows: admin and manager.

Reserving stock for a PO

Orders → open a PO → Items

  1. Each line shows Free at LS Tech Stores: in stock, not reserved for anyone. Gelco Stores stock never counts.
  2. Press Reserve. Whole reels are set aside, oldest stock first, until the line is covered. The last reel may cover a little more than needed.
  3. Press Release to make the reels free stock again.

Reserved reels are protected: Outward warns before shipping one to a different customer, and Transfer will not move one to Gelco. Cancelling or deleting a PO releases its reels automatically.

Admin and manager.

Procurement orders

Orders → Procurement

Create an order from what's short

  1. Open To procure. It lists every item open POs still need after reservations, added up across all POs. Click an item to see which POs are waiting.
  2. If an item has free stock, reserve it on the PO first.
  3. Tick the items to buy and press Create draft order.
  4. Enter the supplier, the expected arrival date and, if you like, a unit cost and currency. The quantity starts at what the POs need. Raise it to buy extra for LS stock, or add other items for stock only.
  5. Press Create draft. When you've placed the order with the supplier, open it and press Mark ordered.

Update progress per item

Open the order from Procurement orders and change each item's stage as it progresses. The customer PO lines waiting on that item move to the same status, on Orders and on the customer portal.

  • EQ
  • RM Procurement
  • Under Manufacturing
  • Under QC
  • In Transit
  • Received

Received is set automatically when the goods are inwarded against the order. An order can be cancelled until anything on it has arrived; the waiting POs go back to "Not planned".

View: admin, manager, staff. Create, edit and change stages: admin and manager.

Receiving goods against an order

Inward

  1. Choose the item as usual. If an open procurement order includes it, an Against procurement order box appears.
  2. Pick the order, enter the reels and submit.

The new reels go into LS Tech Stores and are reserved to the waiting POs, earliest due date first. Anything left over is free stock. When the full quantity has arrived the item is marked Received, and when every item has, the order closes.

Staff inwards still go for approval; the reservation happens when a manager approves.

Shipping reserved reels

Outward

A reserved reel shows Reserved · PO … in the cart. Shipping it on its own PO is normal and completes that line.

If you ship it on a different PO, the system stops and names the PO it's reserved for. Admins and managers can confirm and ship anyway; the reservation is used up.

Gelco transfers and invoices

Transfer · Outward · Docs → Sales

Transferring to Gelco Stores

When the destination is Gelco Stores, the Transfer page shows each item's invoice details. Every item needs a rate and an HSN code before it can move; admins and managers can fill missing ones right there. The details are saved on each reel at that moment, so a later price change does not affect stock already at Gelco.

Invoices for Gelco shipments

  • Each Gelco shipment downloads its tax invoice right after the packing list, with the same number. It is the series Tally uses for Gelco.
  • The layout matches Tally's invoice: CGST/SGST per line, rounded total, amount in words, bank details.
  • Download any shipment's invoice again from the Invoice button in Outward's recent list, or from Docs → Sales.
  • An invoice prints DRAFT while any item has no rate. Admins and managers fill it in on Docs → Sales; tick "use as defaults" to save the details for future invoices.
Still manual: enter the invoice in Tally as before. Pushing it into Tally automatically comes later.

Gelco staff can generate and download invoices but cannot edit them. Editing and setting rates: admin and manager.

Docs

Docs (the old Gelco PDF page is now "Gelco Docs")

Everything from the old CRM's Docs page now lives here, with the same tabs.

  • All shows every document and PO with backlog figures. Press Add to upload a PDF for the AI to read, or enter one by hand.
  • Invoices covers purchase and freight invoices: GST credit, cost per piece, shipment details.
  • Bank has statements per bank, transactions with notes and attached receipts.
  • PO covers customer POs: create, import from a PDF, confirm, dispatch, cancel.
  • Sales holds Gelco tax invoices (see above).

Reviewing an uploaded document

  1. Upload the PDF and wait while it is read, about a minute. You can continue in the background.
  2. The review panel opens with the PDF beside the fields. Check the party, numbers and lines; for purchase invoices, pick the Tally stock item for any line marked ⚠.
  3. Watch for "Possible duplicate" and "Ledger not in Tally" warnings.
  4. Save, then Approve → Tally. The office PC pushes approved documents into Tally.

Upload, edit and save: admin, manager, staff. Approve, reject and delete: admin and manager.

Finance

Finance

The old CRM's Finance page, read from Tally's data. Receivables covers ageing, the collections forecast and debtor reconciliation. Sales Register lets you open any voucher and set a payment reminder date, which creates a task. Inventory shows Tally stock items and valuation. Position has cash and bank, advances, tax and this year's revenue against costs. Payables has supplier balances and the purchase register.

Admin, manager, staff.

What customers see

Customer login → Orders tab

Customers see each of their POs with a status for every line, a progress bar of what has shipped, how much is ready, and the expected date when goods are on the way. Tapping a line shows its steps. They can download documents you attached to the PO (PO, packing list, invoice). They never see suppliers, costs or your stock figures.
